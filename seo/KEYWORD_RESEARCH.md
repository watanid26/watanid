# Keyword Research

**No exact search volumes or difficulty scores appear in this document.** No keyword tool was
available, so every judgement below is qualitative and labelled. Treat it as a hypothesis set to
validate with Search Console once it is collecting data — not as a forecast.

Columns: **Intent** (what the searcher wants) · **Fit** (how well the product actually answers it)
· **Difficulty** (qualitative) · **Conversion** (likelihood of an install) · **Effort** (to produce
the page).

## Kanji 2136

The strongest commercial asset: live on both stores, already running paid Instagram traffic through
`/k2136`, and aimed at KR/JP/SEA learners.

### Primary commercial
| Keyword | Intent | Fit | Difficulty | Conversion | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| joyo kanji app | transactional | high | high — crowded, see competitors | high | low |
| 2136 kanji app | transactional | **exact** | medium | high | low |
| kanji learning app iphone / android | transactional | high | high | high | low |
| 일본 상용한자 앱 | transactional | **exact** | **likely low** — narrow Korean phrasing | high | low |
| 常用漢字 アプリ | transactional | **exact** | medium | high | low |

### Problem-based (the best long-tail for a solo developer)
| Keyword | Intent | Fit | Difficulty | Conversion | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| how many kanji to read a newspaper | informational | high | low | medium | medium |
| jlpt n1 kanji list app | transactional | high | medium | high | low |
| 상용한자 2136자 외우는 법 | informational | high | **low** | medium | medium |
| 漢字 覚え方 アプリ 大人 | informational | high | medium | medium | medium |
| kanji stroke order practice app | transactional | high | medium | high | low |

### Assessment
The generic English head terms ("kanji app") are contested by long-established apps with large
backlink profiles — not winnable soon. **The opening is the Korean and Japanese phrasing**, which is
exactly what the site currently cannot serve to a crawler (audit C3). `일본 상용한자` is close to a
literal product name and is unlikely to be heavily optimised for by the global competitors.

Do not translate the English terms literally. Korean learners search `일본 상용한자` / `상용한자 2136`,
not a transliteration of "joyo kanji".

## Glassframe

Newest product, live on the Chrome Web Store, no landing page at all. Competitors here are actively
doing content marketing (see `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`), which means the queries are real.

### Primary commercial
| Keyword | Intent | Fit | Difficulty | Conversion | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| picture in picture over fullscreen mac | transactional | **exact** | medium | high | low |
| always on top video mac | transactional | high | medium | high | low |
| transparent video overlay mac | transactional | **exact — the differentiator** | **low** | high | low |
| float youtube over fullscreen app | transactional | high | medium | high | low |
| mac 영상 항상 위에 띄우기 | transactional | high | **low** | high | low |

### Problem-based
| Keyword | Intent | Fit | Difficulty | Conversion | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| watch youtube while coding mac | informational | high | medium | high | medium |
| chrome pip disappears in fullscreen | informational | **exact pain point** | low | high | medium |
| how to watch video while working mac | informational | high | medium | medium | medium |
| 作業しながら動画を見る mac | informational | high | low | medium | medium |

### Assessment
**Transparency is the defensible angle.** Every competitor found offers an opaque always-on-top
window; Glassframe's 10–100% opacity overlay is a genuinely different product, and
"transparent/see-through video overlay" looks far less contested than "always on top".

"chrome pip disappears in fullscreen" is the highest-value query in this set: it describes the exact
failure Glassframe fixes, the searcher has already hit the problem, and a short honest how-to page
answers it completely.

## What to do first

1. **Validate before investing.** Get Search Console collecting, wait 4–6 weeks, then read which of
   these queries actually produce impressions. Everything above is a hypothesis.
2. **Write the KR/JP pages only after locale URLs exist** (audit C3). Until then no translated page
   can be crawled, so translating first wastes the work.
3. **Start with the two exact-match, low-difficulty terms** — `transparent video overlay mac` and
   `일본 상용한자 앱`. Both are narrow, both describe the product honestly, and neither requires
   outranking an incumbent.
